Use Power BI. If you're mostly on Microsoft 365, it costs less, fits your existing setup, and suits self-service work better.

- **Cost:** Power BI Pro is included in Microsoft 365 E5. Even bought on its own, it costs much less per user than Tableau Creator or Explorer licenses, and the difference grows fast in a broad rollout.
- **Integration:** It already works with Entra ID for sign-in and permissions, Teams, SharePoint, Excel and Purview labels. Tableau needs more connecting work to get the same results.
- **Familiarity:** Power Query and the Excel-like feel make it easier for business users to get started.

Tableau still wins on visual exploration and polish, so it's worth a look if analysts who do deep, ad hoc visual analysis are your main users.

The main trap with Power BI is skipping governance. Without certified shared datasets and workspace rules from the start, self-service turns into hundreds of duplicate datasets with conflicting numbers. Also check whether you need Fabric capacity for large datasets or for sharing with users who don't have Pro licenses.
