Focus your week on the four areas most data science interviews test, weighted by the job description.

1. **SQL.** Practice joins, window functions (`ROW_NUMBER`, `LAG`), `GROUP BY` with `HAVING`, and CTEs on LeetCode or StrataScratch. This is the most common screen, and people often fail it on NULL handling.
2. **Statistics and experiments.** Review hypothesis tests, p-values, confidence intervals, power, and A/B test pitfalls such as peeking and multiple comparisons.
3. **Machine learning.** Be ready to explain the bias-variance tradeoff, regularization, how to pick evaluation metrics (precision and recall on imbalanced data), and how to prevent leakage.
4. **Product case.** Practice questions like "Engagement dropped 10%; why?" Start by clarifying the metric, then segment the data, then form hypotheses.
5. **Your projects.** Prepare two stories in STAR format (situation, task, action, result), each with a measurable impact. Expect follow-ups on why you chose that approach.
