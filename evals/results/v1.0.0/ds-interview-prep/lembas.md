Put most of your week into statistics and machine learning, since those are where candidates coming from BI usually get caught out, and keep SQL sharp with daily practice.

1. Statistics: review hypothesis tests, p-values, confidence intervals and A/B test design, including pitfalls like peeking and underpowered tests.
2. Machine learning: be ready to explain bias versus variance, regularization, data leakage, and how you would choose and evaluate a model for a business problem.
3. SQL: solve a few timed problems each day with joins, window functions and aggregations.
4. Python: clean and summarize a messy dataset in pandas without looking up syntax.
5. Stories: prepare three STAR examples where your analysis changed a decision, with the numbers.
6. Company: study their product and have two or three metrics you would track for it.

Two days before, do a full mock interview out loud, including one case question where you talk through your approach.
