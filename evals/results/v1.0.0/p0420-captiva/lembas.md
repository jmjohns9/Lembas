P0420 means "Catalyst System Efficiency Below Threshold (Bank 1)": the downstream oxygen sensor is seeing nearly the same oxygen swings as the upstream one, so the computer thinks the catalytic converter is no longer cleaning the exhaust well. It is usually safe to keep driving for now if the check engine light is steady and the car runs normally.

Stop driving and get it checked soon if any of these show up:
- The check engine light flashes, which means an active misfire that can overheat and destroy the converter.
- You smell rotten eggs or hear a rattle under the car.
- The engine loses power or struggles on hills, which points to a clogged converter.

Check the cheaper causes before buying a converter:
1. Exhaust leak ahead of the rear O2 sensor, often at the flex pipe or gaskets.
2. Failing downstream O2 sensor.
3. Misfires or a rich condition (look for other stored codes like P0300 through P0304).
4. A worn converter, the most common cause on a 2014 with high mileage.

Fix the root cause first, or a new converter will fail too.
